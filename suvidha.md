## Problem

- People in Assam may not know which government schemes could help them.
- Scheme information is spread across different sources.
- Eligibility rules can be difficult to understand.

## Solution: Suvidha

- A website focused on discovering government schemes in Assam.
- Search by keyword or answer a few questions.
- See schemes that may be relevant and why.
- Get links to official information and application pages.
- Final eligibility is decided by the government department.

## Two core features

- **Search and browse:** Find schemes by need, keyword, category, or location.
- **Guided matching:** Answer relevant questions to see potential schemes and requirements to check.

## MVP

- Start with a small, verified catalogue in one or two categories.
- Include keyword search and simple filters.
- Add a short questionnaire and potential match results.
- Show benefits, eligibility, required documents, application steps, official links, and last-verified dates.
- Do not submit applications or guarantee eligibility.

## Product moat

- A trusted, Assam-focused scheme catalogue.
- Clear explanations of why each scheme may match.
- A reliable process for checking and updating information.
- Local language and accessibility support.
